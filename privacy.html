Privacy policy

Fitbit Air Health Exporter · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Fitbit Air Health Exporter ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its author to back up their own data. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Health data, read-only: activity and fitness (such as steps, heart rate, exercise), health metrics and measurements (such as weight, blood oxygen, temperature), sleep, nutrition, ECG recordings and irregular rhythm notifications, as made available by the Google Health API for the signed-in account.
  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (the drive.file permission): the app creates one folder and writes the backup files into it. It cannot read, change or delete any other Drive files.

How the data is used

The app's only purpose is to copy the signed-in user's own health data into that user's own Google Drive, unmodified, so the user keeps a complete personal archive. The data is not used for any other purpose.

Where the data is stored and processed

The app runs only in computing environments controlled by its user (their own computer or a cloud development environment they control). Data passes through that environment while being copied and is stored only in the user's own Google Drive. The app has no servers or databases of its own.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone. It shows no ads and does not use the data for advertising, credit decisions or training AI models.

Limited Use

Fitbit Air Health Exporter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The backup files remain in the user's Google Drive until the user deletes them. Deleting the app's Drive folder removes all stored data. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer read any data.

Security

Access uses Google's OAuth sign-in with read-only health permissions. Sign-in credentials are kept in the user's private environment settings and are never published.
