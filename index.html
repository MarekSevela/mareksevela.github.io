Fitbit Air Health Exporter

A personal tool by GitHub user MarekSevela.

Fitbit Air Health Exporter makes a private backup of its owner's own health and fitness data. It reads the owner's data through the Google Health API (read-only) and saves an unmodified copy as files in the owner's own Google Drive, so the owner can keep and analyze their complete history.

Who can use it

It is not a public service. Only its author uses it, with their own Google account. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no website login.

What it accesses

  • Google Health data (read-only): activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, sleep, nutrition, ECG and irregular rhythm notifications.
  • Google Drive (only files it creates): to store the backup files. It cannot see or change any other files in Drive.

Details are in the privacy policy.
